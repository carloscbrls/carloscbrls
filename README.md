# Carlos Cabrales III (C3)

I build practical AI systems for small businesses and nonprofits in California's Central Valley — and I make music as **CC3PO**.

This GitHub account is the workshop behind two companies:

- **CC3PO** — AI automation, websites, and training. We design and build AI agents that handle real work for small teams: answering questions, organizing information, preparing documents, and keeping routine operations moving.
- **CCFamily Communications** — the communications and media side of the work, including web design and content for local organizations.

## What we build

- **AI agents and automation for small business and nonprofits** — working systems, set up and documented so a small team can actually use them.
- **The Applied AI & Automation Lab** — a hands-on training program launching January 2027 in the Central Valley. Participants learn by building; every session ends with something that runs. Apply at [academy.cc3po.com](https://academy.cc3po.com).
- **Websites** — fast, static sites for our own properties and for clients, mostly built with Astro and published through Netlify.
- **CC3PO music** — original albums and singles written and produced in the Central Valley, at [music.cc3po.com](https://music.cc3po.com).

## How these repositories are organized

- **Live websites** — one repository per site, named after its address (for example, `academy-cc3po-astro` is academy.cc3po.com). Tagged `site-cc3po`.
- **Apps and tools** — compliance tools, scanners, dashboards, portals, and builders. Tagged `app`.
- **Lab and learning** — course material for the AI Builders program. Tagged `lab`.
- **Music and personal brand** — the music site, Don't Lose Your Way, and Third & C3, my fantasy football desk. Tagged `music` or `personal-brand`.

Client websites also live in this account. They are listed for clarity only and are not open projects.
